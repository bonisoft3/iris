# Acceptance

What "done" looks like, one check per line. Each id below is cited by the
invariants in [ir.html](ir.html) that realize it; coverage is a lint, not a hope.

- Iris opens directly with a lazy guest session and an on-demand Access & Portability modal offering Passkey, Google OIDC, and device-bound guest access. {#accept-door}
- Sign in with Google on the deployed site leaves for the provider's door: the edge's start route exists because the release names the broker and client. {#accept-signin-google}
- Signing in or committing a guest session names the reader in the navigation rail beside sign out and lets a guest promote their session to a Passkey or Google account in place. {#accept-signin-gallery}
- A fresh Iris database already holds a living circular-recovery network: twelve classified waste specimens across all five categories (`recyclable`, `organic`, `eletronic`, `non_recyclable`, `trash`), four neighbourhood disposal places, and localized translations. {#accept-demo-items}
- The gallery lists public non-flagged waste specimens newest first, each showing its specimen picture, caption, waste category badge (`recyclable`, `organic`, `eletronic`, `non_recyclable`, `trash`), material summary, and a link to its detail page (`/images/:id`). {#accept-gallery-grid}
- The gallery offers a view switcher between "IRIS Global" (the community feed of public specimens) and "My Items" (the signed-in reader's personal scan requests and classified specimens). {#accept-gallery-tabs}
- Selecting "My Items" displays the reader's scan requests with each request's 4-step status phase track (`submitted`, `synced`, `classifying`, `classified`) and city, alongside the reader's own classified waste specimens. {#accept-my-items-phases}
- Clicking "Preview in panel" on any gallery specimen card updates the desktop inspection side panel on `/` to show that specimen's picture, waste category badge, material, numbered disposal instructions, and a direct link to `/images/:id` without leaving the gallery. {#accept-gallery-preview}
- The gallery (`/`) offers waste-category filter chips (`all`, `recyclable`, `organic`, `eletronic`, `non_recyclable`, `trash`) and a live specimen search bar (`#gallery-search` with `#search-clear` reset) driven by `GalleryView` and `gallery_view.js`, filtering the community and personal specimen grids in place by waste category (`data-category-filter="{category}"`) and case-insensitive caption match (`caption=ilike.*{query}*`). {#accept-gallery-search-filter}
- An item's detail page (`/images/:id`) shows its full picture, caption, primary waste category badge, object material, step-by-step disposal instructions, sub-classifications, estimated recovery value, nearby recycling places, and a link back to the gallery. {#accept-detail-page}
- The item detail page offers three inspection tabs (`How to Discard`, `Material & Streams`, and `Nearby Centers`), displaying localized disposal translations (`en`, `es`, `pt`, `ru`, `he`, `kk`) on the discard tab and matching neighbourhood drop-off hubs (`DisposalPlace`) filtered by the specimen's waste stream on the nearby tab. {#accept-detail-tabs}
- An item address (`/images/:id`) that no specimen carries states plainly that no item was found, with a link back to the gallery rather than a blank screen. {#accept-detail-unknown}
- First-time visitors on `/` see the 3-step "Como funciona" onboarding walkthrough (`OnboardingStep` with `durability: "device"` and `visited = 'false'`), stepping through three illustrated slides and setting `visited = 'true'` on Skip or Start Iris now to reveal the gallery permanently on that device, while `/intro` remains accessible on demand as a non-menu route (`strip: false`). {#accept-intro-stepper}
- The About page (`/about`) presents Iris's product overview with fanned specimen cards, a 3-step "How it works" summary, direct links to the ad campaign landing pages (`/lp`) and onboarding walkthrough (`/intro`), and the `bonisoft³.` studio attribution card. {#accept-about-page}
- The campaigns showcase (`/lp`) serves as a non-menu ad landing page (`strip: false`) linked from `/about`, presenting Iris's three acquisition tracks (`Snap & Recycle`, `Small Choices, Big Impact`, `AI for Recycling`) alongside live classified community specimens and CTAs into `/intro` and `/`. {#accept-landing-campaigns}
- The camera scan screen (`/camera`) mounts the `camera-lens` isolated unit for live camera capture and interactive MediaPipe click-to-segment object isolation, classifies specimens with Gemini 2.5 Flash (`model = 'gemini'`) in the reader's active locale via `ScanDraft`, and submits a `TrackRequest` with their collection city. {#accept-camera-scan}
- Tapping an object in the `camera-lens` unit on `/camera` runs in-browser MediaPipe `InteractiveSegmenter` (`magic_touch.tflite`) and emits a `capture` event handled by `scan_draft.js` to update `ScanDraft.image_url`, `segment_region`, and `segment_status`. {#accept-camera-segmentation}
- Submitting a scan request on `/camera` triggers deterministic specimen classification (`015_classify_request.sql`), promoting the `TrackRequest` to `status = 'classified'` and inserting a corresponding `TrashItem` row keyed idempotently by `request_id` so the newly classified specimen appears in the gallery. {#accept-classify-trigger}
- Submitting a translation request on `/images/:id` (`request-translation` form) triggers deterministic multilingual translation (`016_translate_item.sql`), populating or updating the matching `(item_id, language)` row in `TrashItemTranslation` so localized disposal instructions appear immediately on the **How to Discard** tab. {#accept-translation-trigger}
- Every screen uses `container-type: inline-size` and `@container` responsive breakpoints (`390px` mobile, `768px` tablet, `1280px` desktop) with zero horizontal overflow, enforces touch-target heights (`--control-h` / `--control-h-sm`), and reserves explicit `width`, `height`, and `aspect-ratio` layout-shift guards on every specimen, hub, campaign, and onboarding image. {#accept-responsive-geometry}
- All six locale catalogues (`en`, `es`, `pt`, `ru`, `he`, `kk`) carry identical key sets, non-empty localized copy in their target scripts, and matching `{placeholder}` variables across every screen and route label, and Hebrew (`/he`) renders with right-to-left (`dir="rtl"`) logical inline CSS layout and bidi-isolated Hebrew specimen translations without horizontal overflow. {#accept-six-locales-rtl}
- Every specimen picture, drop-off hub icon, campaign visual, onboarding illustration, and classification stream icon the demo network ships is served by Iris itself, content-addressed under `/pictures/<sha256>.(svg|png)`, with its SHA-256 digest, source, license, and author recorded in `assets/manifest.json` (39 entries). {#accept-specimens-shipped}
- Every screen reads clearly and meets WCAG AA contrast (>= 4.5:1) in both the device's light and dark appearance via `light-dark()` tokens in `shell/design.css`, declares a `populated-dark` state, and contains zero raw hex or `rgb()`/`hsl()`/`oklch()` colour literals or unresolved custom properties in authored screen or shared stylesheets. {#accept-dual-appearance}
