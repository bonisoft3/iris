iris — engineering design

Compiled from brief.md. Nine cluster entities plus the tab-scoped GalleryView workspace on the mecha cluster, interactive gallery view switching and desktop inspection panel in Turn 2, content-addressed static specimens.

Overview

Iris is a multilingual waste-sorting lens and circular-recovery field guide converted from guis/iris and guis/web. The terminal owns the sign-in door (decision 1): a passkey or a one-click guest session, gating every screen. Public tables carry additive owner-write policies and explicit column grants in 011_owner_writes.sql (decision 2), while Me is a private row provisioned by 013_me_provision.sql on AppUser insert and narrowed read-only by 012_me_readonly.sql (decision 3). A fresh database starts populated with twelve curated waste specimens across all five waste streams, localized translations across six languages, four neighbourhood disposal places, two reusable donations, and six environmental news items (decision 4), all referencing content-addressed SVG/PNG specimens under /pictures/ (decision 5). Turn 2 adds the tab-scoped GalleryView workspace entity and gallery_view reduce handler (decision 8) so the gallery switches cleanly between IRIS Global and My Items (with 4-phase TrackRequest status tracks) and drives a live desktop specimen preview panel alongside the grid.

Auth

auth service · mecha cluster

The terminal's gate markup is styled as a split page in shell/shared/chrome.css (decision 1): a deep ocean navy panel (var(--header)) carrying the Iris mission beside the passkey/guest sign-in form on the pale-mint ground (var(--neutral)). The mecha auth service mints (id, handle) into AppUser, and the 013_me_provision.sql trigger provisions the corresponding Me row in the same transaction.

          1. Iris uses lazy guest sessions with auth.required: false, promote: true, and Google OIDC (identity_signin.cue). Readers enter the gallery or scan screen immediately without a blocking gate; an on-demand Access & Portability modal lets them commit their device-bound guest session or promote it in place to a Passkey or Google account without losing their scanned specimens.
          2. Public entities with owner-scoped writes use access: public plus additive RLS policies and column grants in 011_owner_writes.sql. #Access has no "everyone reads, owner writes" mode, and widening a private table in SQL would violate the browser mirror contract. Narrowing write columns in 011_owner_writes.sql keeps handle, created_at, and trigger-populated tables safe.
          3. Me is a live, private one-row table provisioned synchronously by trigger 013_me_provision.sql. Because Omnishell templates have no session variable placeholder, a private me row scoped to auth_uid() serves as the universal ownership probe across screens.
          4. A fresh volume starts with twelve classified waste specimens, four disposal places, translations, and news in 020_demo_items.sql. An empty recycling guide cannot demonstrate sorting streams, material recovery values, or multilingual instructions to a first-time guest.
